PART I — PRIVACY POLICY

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how pazoskill ("pazoskill," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information obtained from users of our website and services, including course enrollment, certificate issuance, and job-placement assistance (collectively, the "Services"). By accessing or using the Services, you ("User," "Student," or "you") acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, please do not access or use the Services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

(a) Information You Provide Directly

  • Full name, email address, phone number, and mailing/billing address
  • Account credentials (username and password)
  • Payment and billing information (processed by a third-party payment processor; see Section 5)
  • Educational background, employment history, and portfolio materials submitted for job-placement assistance
  • Communications you send to us (support tickets, feedback, correspondence)

(b) Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and click-stream data
  • Course progress, quiz/assessment results, and certificate completion records
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

(c) Information from Third Parties

  • Verification data from identity or payment verification providers
  • Information from employers or partner organizations in connection with job-placement services, where applicable

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for purposes including, without limitation, to:

  1. Create and administer your account and deliver course content;
  2. Process payments and issue receipts or invoices;
  3. Track course progress and issue certificates of completion;
  4. Facilitate job-placement assistance, including sharing relevant materials with prospective employers or partners with your consent;
  5. Communicate with you regarding your account, course updates, and administrative matters;
  6. Improve, personalize, and analyze the performance of our Services;
  7. Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity;
  8. Comply with applicable legal obligations, regulatory requirements, and enforceable governmental requests.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis for processing personal data, we rely on one or more of the following: (a) your consent; (b) the necessity of processing to perform our contract with you (e.g., delivering purchased courses); (c) compliance with a legal obligation; and/or (d) our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Services, provided such interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers, including payment processors, cloud hosting providers, and email/communication platforms, solely to the extent necessary to provide the Services;
  • Employer/hiring partners, only where you have opted into job-placement assistance and only to the extent necessary to facilitate that assistance;
  • Legal and regulatory authorities, where required to comply with applicable law, court order, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of pazoskill Academy, our users, or the public;
  • Successors in interest, in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality obligations.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Services, remember your preferences, and analyze usage patterns. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies; however, doing so may limit your ability to use certain features of the Services.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Course completion and certificate records may be retained indefinitely for verification purposes.

8. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to: (a) access the personal information we hold about you; (b) request correction of inaccurate information; (c) request deletion of your information; (d) object to or restrict certain processing; (e) request portability of your data; and (f) withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. 10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly.

14. General Statement — No Refunds

Except as expressly set forth in Section 16 below or as required by applicable law, all payments made to pazoskill for courses, certificates, subscriptions, job-placement services, or any other paid offering are final and non-refundable. By completing a purchase, you expressly acknowledge and agree that you are not entitled to a refund, credit, or exchange for any reason, including but not limited to: dissatisfaction with course content, failure to complete a course, failure to obtain employment following completion, change of personal circumstances, or lack of use of purchased access.

15. Rationale for This Policy

This no-refund policy exists because, upon enrollment, you are granted immediate access to proprietary digital course materials, video content, downloadable resources, and/or a license to use such materials. This access constitutes performance of our obligations under the contract of sale at the moment it is granted, and the digital nature of the content means it cannot be "returned" in the manner of a physical good. Accordingly, consideration is deemed fully exchanged upon the grant of access, regardless of whether you subsequently choose to consume the content in full, in part, or not at all.

16. Limited Exceptions

Notwithstanding Section 14, a refund may be considered, at pazoskill's sole discretion, only in the following limited circumstances:

(a) Duplicate or erroneous charges resulting from a technical or billing error;
(b) Non-delivery of purchased access due to a verified technical fault attributable solely to pazoskill, provided you notify us within 7 days of purchase and the issue is not resolved within a reasonable time;
(c) Mandatory statutory rights that cannot be excluded under the consumer protection laws of your jurisdiction of residence, which may include a short "cooling-off" period for certain online purchases. Nothing in this Policy is intended to limit or exclude any right that applicable law does not permit us to exclude.

Any request for a refund under this Section must be submitted in writing to the contact details in Section 13 within 7 days of the date of purchase, accompanied by a description of the issue. Requests submitted outside this window, or that do not meet the criteria above, will not be honored.

Such certificates attest only to completion of coursework and do not constitute a professional license, accredited academic degree, or guarantee of any kind. No refund will be issued on the basis that a Student did not secure employment following participation in job-placement services.
